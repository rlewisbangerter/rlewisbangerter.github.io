# The Invisible Chain

**Live page:** https://reubenbangerter.org/invisible-chain/

A research project on the invisible human cost of global logistics: why physical labor is systematically undervalued in global supply chains, using the Smile Curve to trace where value goes. By Reuben Bangerter (Rutgers, Business Analytics & IT / Supply Chain Management), Apr – May 2026. Originally published as a Google Site.

## Sections
- **Hero:** central question and the Smile Curve
- **A feature, not a bug:** stance and author note
- **Background:** the Smile Curve, key terms, the "one-click" world
- **Data:** Baldwin & Ito (2021), Maya Aktar's testimony, AI data labor
- **Solutions:** worker networks, three policy proposals, the efficiency vs. equity debate
- **Breaking the chain:** reflections and organizations to follow
- **Works cited** (APA 7)
